Ticket Tycoon privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Ticket Tycoon is a game by Coffee Cup Solutions Ltd. This policy is short because there is very little to say.

What we collect

Nothing. Ticket Tycoon does not collect, store or send any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your phone

Your progress and your settings are saved on your device so the game can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple services

If a future version saves your progress to iCloud or shows achievements through Game Center, that data is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. It goes between your device and your Apple account only and never reaches us.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the new version is released.
